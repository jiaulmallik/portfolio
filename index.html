🔴 The challenge

Fragmented funnel, low attendance, no automation

Isolated paid campaigns, no unified landing page strategy, no CRM integration, and a webinar show-up rate of only 18% — below the 22% industry average. Leads were captured but never nurtured; sales had no visibility on lead quality.

🟡 The strategy

Full-funnel event engine with CRM at the core

A multi-channel acquisition funnel feeding a HubSpot-powered nurture system. LinkedIn targeted VP/Director/C-suite by title and company size, Google Search captured intent, Meta handled retargeting — every channel fed the same landing page, workflow, and lead score model.

🟢 The outcome

$100M+ pipeline, 45% show-up rate, 382 MQLs

The rebuilt funnel delivered 4,241 leads per campaign cycle, hit 45% registration-to-attendance conversion, and contributed over $100M in influenced pipeline. Post-event sequences recovered 18% of no-shows; retargeting cut drop-offs 31%.

Step-by-step execution
WEEK 1–2

ICP Research & Audience Architecture

Analysed customer data to define the ICP — enterprise IT teams (500+ employees), US-based, with Azure or Fabric in their stack. Built 6 LinkedIn audience segments by job title + seniority + company size and custom intent audiences in Google Ads.

Landing Page & Funnel Build
